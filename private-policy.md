<h1>Privacy Policy</h1>
<p>Options Exposure for Fidelity. Effective November 8, 2026.</p>
<p>This is an independent browser extension, not affiliated with, endorsed by, or produced by Fidelity Investments or Google.</p>
<h2>Information Handled</h2>
<p>Only after you agree to the in-extension disclosure and click Scan Portfolio, the extension reads the rendered Options sections on the active Fidelity Positions page. It handles account labels and identifiers, underlying tickers, option expiration dates, strikes, types, quantities, cost basis, current values, and gain/loss. It also handles the options cash budget you enter and your display and account-selection settings.</p>
<p>The extension does not request your Fidelity password, read authentication cookies, access Fidelity account APIs, place trades, or monitor your browsing history. It does not collect analytics or use advertising, tracking pixels, or a developer-operated backend.</p>
<h2>Local Storage and Encryption</h2>
<p>Your budget, portfolio snapshots, account settings, preferences, saved quotes and consent record are stored in an encrypted vault in this Chrome profile using AES-256-GCM. A key is derived from your local four-digit numeric PIN using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA-256 with 600,000 iterations and a random salt. Each save uses a new random IV. The PIN is not saved or sent to external services. The unlock key is held in extension memory and Chrome's restricted session storage, not persisted beside the encrypted vault. Every newly opened popup requires the PIN before it can display saved financial data or access protected storage. A cached internal key lets already-requested background quotes finish after the popup closes; it does not bypass the PIN prompt on reopening. Data is not synced by the extension.</p>
<p>A four-digit PIN provides limited protection against offline guessing and is weaker than six digits or a long passphrase. Encryption does not protect a compromised computer, an authenticated popup, screenshots, malware, or copies in device backups. Existing unencrypted data from older versions is migrated after consent and PIN setup; old extension-storage records and the legacy local budget are then removed. Existing passphrase or six-digit PIN vaults require that previous credential once to re-encrypt with the new four-digit PIN, preserving data. Deleting records cannot guarantee forensic erasure from operating-system backups or storage media.</p>
<h2>Optional Stock Prices and Google</h2>
<p>Stock prices are off by default. Enabling them requests optional access to https://www.google.com. Chrome host permissions apply to the whole origin, even though this extension only fetches Google Finance quote pages. When enabled, a Scan Portfolio click sends underlying ticker symbols and candidate exchange codes to Google Finance over HTTPS to obtain stock-price snapshots. The requests omit Google cookies and referrers and do not include your option details, quantities, account identifiers, Fidelity page URL or budget. Opening the popup does not start these requests.</p>
<p>Google receives the requested symbols, your IP address and normal HTTP/browser metadata. These requests are not anonymous. Google's handling of requests is governed by the <a href="https://policies.google.com/privacy" rel="noreferrer">Google Privacy Policy</a>. Public pages may be delayed, blocked, unsupported or changed; there is no guaranteed live quote service.</p>
<p>Clicking a company ticker opens a normal Google search tab for that ticker plus "stock". Normal browser and Google cookie/settings behavior applies to that user-opened tab, unlike the cookie-free background quote requests.</p>
<h2>Use, Sharing and Retention</h2>
<p>Information is used only to display your options, calculate estimates, retrieve optional quotes, and preserve your chosen local settings. The developer does not receive your portfolio or budget, and does not sell or transfer them to advertisers, data brokers or other parties. The only external data requests made by the extension are the optional Google quote requests described above and links you explicitly open.</p>
<p>Saved data remains until replaced by a later scan, deleted with Clear Saved Data, or removed when you uninstall the extension. Quote snapshots may remain older than your latest scan when a lookup fails. Disabling Stock Prices stops quote refreshes and removes optional Google access; existing encrypted quotes remain visible until deleted.</p>
<h2>Your Controls</h2>
<p>You may decline setup by closing the popup, disable Stock Prices, lock the vault, edit your budget, choose which accounts affect calculations, or use Clear Saved Data to delete the vault, settings, consent record, popup authorizations and session key. Deletion does not change your Fidelity account or erase Google's records. A forgotten PIN cannot be recovered; resetting the vault permanently deletes saved extension data.</p>
<h2>Limited Use and Changes</h2>
<p>The extension's use of user data complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Data is used only for the disclosed user-facing purpose. Material changes to these practices will be described in the extension before any new use requiring consent.</p>
<h2>Contact</h2>
<p>Contact the publisher through the Support contact shown on this extension's Chrome Web Store listing. Do not send passwords, account numbers, or unredacted portfolio screenshots.</p>
